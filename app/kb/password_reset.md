# Password Reset and Account Lockout

Users can request a password reset from the sign-in page. Reset links expire after 30 minutes and can only be used once. If a reset email does not arrive, confirm the email address, ask the user to check spam/quarantine, and allow-list the product email domain.

After five failed sign-in attempts, an account may be temporarily locked for 15 minutes. Repeated lockouts or inaccessible email accounts should be routed to the Account Team.
