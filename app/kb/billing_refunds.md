# Billing, Duplicate Charges, and Refund Requests

For an unexpected or duplicate charge, collect the invoice ID, charge date, last four digits of the payment method if the customer is comfortable sharing them, and the workspace name. Do not ask for full card numbers or CVV codes.

Billing disputes, refund requests, and duplicate charges must be routed to the Billing Team. Support agents should not promise that a refund has been approved until the Billing Team confirms it.
